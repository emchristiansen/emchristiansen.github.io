Privacy policy: Personal administration

Last updated 2026-09-27.

Who runs it

Personal administration is a private tool that Eric Christiansen runs for his own use. Only his own Google account uses it, and it is not offered as a public service.

What Google data it accesses

  • His Google account's email address and basic profile.
  • Gmail: his mail. The permission covers reading, labelling, drafting and sending mail. Agents are instructed to send a message only with his explicit approval for that message.
  • Google Calendar: his calendars and events. The permission covers reading, creating, changing, sharing and deleting them.
  • Google Drive: his files and their details. The permission covers reading, creating, changing, sharing and deleting them.

How the data is used

The data is used only to let software tools and AI agents working for him read, organize and act on his own mail, calendar and files, at his direction.

Who else receives it

The content that agents read is processed by the AI services that run those agents, currently Anthropic and OpenAI, only to carry out his requests. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else, except with the people he chooses when he sends a message or shares a file or event, where the law requires it, or to investigate security problems. This tool does not use the data to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. Whether an AI service may use the content it processes is governed by his own account settings with that service.

Storage and security

The Google authorization is stored encrypted in a password manager and used only on his own computer. Data travels over encrypted connections. Copies of content that agents read may remain in working files on his own computer, and in the AI services' records.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops new access; it does not delete copies already made. There is no fixed retention period: copies on his computer stay until they are deleted from that computer, and records held by an AI service follow that service's retention rules, and he can use the deletion controls that service provides.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If the way this tool uses Google data changes, this policy will be updated, with a new date, before the data is used in the new way.
